About us

Single-family housing is all we do

We are an independent firm focused on generating superior outcomes for our partners and providing our residents with adequate, attainable housing.

What we do

A simpler path to owning a home in Texas.

What we do

We offer residents a way into single-family communities in high-growth markets — without a large down payment or a mortgage to start. Through INTRO, we buy the home alongside you. You move in, pay a locked rent, and buy it back at a pre-agreed price, on your timeline.

How we do it

Our in-house technology and operations team runs a fully integrated process: sourcing homes, administering INTRO, and supporting residents from pre-qualification through move-in. Data informs our decisions; custom software underpins asset management and the rent-to-own program.

The market

Housing is a basic need. Access is not keeping up.

Large and resilient

  • The US single-family residential market is over $50 trillion.
  • Single-family rental rates have remained positive through every economic shock since 1985.
  • Home prices have increased at a 4.2% average per year since 1987. Home equity is now the largest driver of wealth in the US.

Supply / demand imbalance

  • Over 30 million Americans between 18–34 are expected to drive record household formation in the next decade.
  • There is currently a ~4 million unit housing shortage in the US (Freddie Mac). Some estimates are as high as 6.5 million.

Widespread affordability issues

  • Rents and home prices continue to outpace wage growth.
  • In each of the 50 largest US cities, the share of renters is larger today than it was 15 years ago.
