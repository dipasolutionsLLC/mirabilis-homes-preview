How it works

Home ownership made easy

Our INTRO program is specifically designed to help first time homebuyers begin their ownership journey without taking on debt or providing a significant down payment.

Become a homeowner in 4 easy steps

01

Apply online

Apply directly on our website. Applying is free and does not impact your credit score.

  • No commitment
  • Receive your budget in 1–3 days
02

Choose your dream home

Work with your preferred agent — or ask us for a referral — to find a home that fits our selection criteria.

03

We buy it alongside you

Our all-cash offers give you an advantage in the bidding process. When Mirabilis closes, you contribute 2% of the purchase price.

04

Lease with an option to buy

You sign a lease at pre-agreed rental rates for up to six years, with a purchase option at prices you already know. Your 2% is credited toward that price.

What are my required payments?

At closing

Initial contribution

Your 2% initial contribution is deposited into your Tenant Fund. If you exercise your purchase option, the entire balance is credited toward the option purchase price.

Each month

Monthly rent

Your only ongoing financial commitment is monthly rent. You are not required to exercise your purchase option or make additional contributions.

Whenever you want

Voluntary contributions

You decide if, when, and how much additional cash you’d like to contribute to your Tenant Fund. The more you contribute, the more financial benefits you receive.

Ready when you are
